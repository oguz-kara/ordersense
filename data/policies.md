# Loura Apparel — support knowledge base (fictional EU demo brand)

## Shipping
- EU (DE/FR/NL/BE): 2–5 business days, free over €80, otherwise €4.95
- Rest of Europe: 5–9 business days, €9.95
- Outside Europe: 8–14 business days, €14.95
- "Don't panic" thresholds: domestic 5 business days, international 12 —
  before that, a moving tracking status is normal.

## Returns & exchanges
- 30 days from delivery, unworn with tags, original packaging preferred
- Free return label for EU orders; refund 5–8 business days after arrival
  at the warehouse
- Exchanges: size swaps free once per order
- Final sale items (marked on product page) cannot be returned

## Sizing
- Fits true to size; between sizes → size up for the Relaxed line,
  size down for the Tailored line
- Size guide on every product page

## Payments
- Cards, PayPal, Klarna (DE/NL), iDEAL (NL)
- Refunds go to the original payment method only

## Contact & languages
- Support hours: Mon–Fri 09:00–18:00 CET; agent covers EN, DE, FR
- WhatsApp and Instagram DM answered by the same assistant
